# FIGHT RULES — hand-to-hand and VFX combat cuts

Read before writing. Run the checklist at the bottom before spending credits.

프롬프트 쓰기 전에 읽고, 돌리기 전에 맨 아래 체크리스트를 봅니다.

## Hard bans

1. **No "hits / strikes / punches / kicks him" sentences.** Write where the body goes and what breaks. A sentence that only says "hits" produces a weak tap. — "때린다" 문장 금지. 이동과 부서지는 것으로 쓴다.
2. **No one-dimensional exchange from a standing spot** (lead swings, opponent reacts, repeat). — 선 자리에서 치고받기 금지.
3. **No slow tempo.** If it could pass for a schoolyard fight, rewrite. — 느린 템포 금지.
4. **No wings sprouting from the back, no separate bird or animal form.** The signature VFX lives in the trail of the blade or limb, and only there. — 등에서 날개 돋기·별도 형체 금지. 잔상은 궤적에만.
5. **No face-breaking angles.** Lead from behind, rear 3/4, or high angle; no frontal or side helmet close-ups unless your tool holds faces; no extreme creature close-ups. — 얼굴 깨질 앵글은 넣지 않는다.
6. **No props you didn't design.** If a sword or chain shows up uninvited, remove the reference that suggested it. — 설계 안 한 칼·사슬 금지.
7. **A cut never ends on its first composition.** — 끝이 첫 구도로 돌아가면 틀림.
8. **The VFX does not go out after an attack.** — 공격 후 불꽃 꺼짐 금지.
9. **One creature per clip** (8 s). Two or more in a 15 s clip broke faces twice. — 괴물 하나당 한 클립.
10. **Nobody bounces off a shield by himself.** The lead sends him flying. — 방어막에 스스로 튕겨나가는 그림 금지.
11. **A projectile arm is not a gun.** It thrusts and sweeps like a spear. — 쏘는 구도 금지. 창처럼 찌르고 휘두른다.

## Beat method

Each beat, in this order:

1. Where the lead moves **from → to** (never "stands and…").
2. **One thing that breaks** in this beat: floor, container, crane, water column.
3. **Camera on a flight path**, moving with or against the action: push, whip-pan, whip-tilt, dutch, orbit, low / high / ground.
4. **1-frame white flash → 2–3-frame hit-stop → physical result**: the opponent flies and embeds, water erupts, sparks.

비트마다: ①어디서 어디로 ②부서지는 것 하나 ③카메라 비행 경로 ④플래시·히트스톱·물리 결과.

Also:

- After the start frame, **hard-cut and change the angle before the first attack.** Never attack from the start composition. — 시작 프레임 뒤 바로 하드컷, 앵글 바꾼 뒤 공격.
- **A miss is an opportunity.** A thrust that misses produces a huge explosion behind the target. — 빗나가면 미친 폭발.
- The attack VFX **leaves a trail every time**; the defense VFX **shows its geometry** (e.g. hex plates), or the audience never learns what it is. — 공격마다 잔상, 방어는 형태가 보여야.
- **8 s = 2–3 big moves.** Camera-angle variety, action and speed change (burst / hit-stop / ramp) happen together. — 8초에 큰 동작 2~3개. 앵글·액션·속도 변화는 세트.
- **Blocked is not finished.** The opponent flies, embeds, drops. — 막히면 끝이 아니라 날아가 박힌다.
- **Multiples are never identical.** Same shape and size, different motion. — 같은 게 여럿이면 모션만 다르게.
- Consecutive strikes: **change the shot between them.** — 연타도 화면 전환 후.
- If the fight has to last (nobody dies, three opponents), alternate two powers and change the angle per exchange instead of adding more hits. — 길게 가야 하면 타격 수가 아니라 능력 교대·앵글 교대.
- The lead stays **slim, never bulky**, whatever the angle. Build drifts wide in full shots unless you say so. — 주인공은 항상 날씬하게.
- Fixed left/right placements stay fixed across angle changes (e.g. the spear-arm creature is always to the lead's left). — 좌우 배치는 앵글이 바뀌어도 유지.

## The bar

If the move would look weak in Naruto, One Piece, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, Gundam, Kaiju No. 8 or Chainsaw Man, rewrite it. For proof that Seedance can reach that level, see Bendo's *Naruto vs Jinwoo* and the Korean creator 영상독학's one-day Seedance fantasy action film.

The script is the forest. The video is allowed to be more spectacular than the script.

기준은 나루토·원피스·귀멸·주술회전급. 소설은 큰 숲이고 영상은 더 화려해도 된다.

## Pre-run checklist

- [ ] No "hits / strikes" sentence anywhere
- [ ] Lead moves from → to in every beat
- [ ] One thing breaks per beat
- [ ] Camera position stated first, then a move per beat
- [ ] Flash + hit-stop + physical result on every contact
- [ ] Hard cut after the start frame, angle changed before the first attack
- [ ] 2–3 big moves for 8 s, not more
- [ ] One creature, one face, headcount with total and positions
- [ ] End frame described and different from the first composition
- [ ] FAIL list written for this cut's specific risks
- [ ] Prompt under 10,000 characters
