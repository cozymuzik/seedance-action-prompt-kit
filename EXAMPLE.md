# EXAMPLE — one complete 8-second action cut

A generic cut that uses every block. Replace names and images with yours.

모든 블록을 쓴 8초짜리 예시입니다. 이름과 이미지만 바꿔 쓰세요.

## Settings note (kept above the prompt, not sent)

- Magnific · Seedance 2.5 · 16:9 · 720p · 8s · audio on
- @Image1 — lead character sheet → DESIGN ONLY
- @Image2 — start-frame still: lead seen from behind, rear 3/4, container yard at night, rain → START FRAME
- @Image3 — creature design sheet → DESIGN ONLY

## Prompt

```
[No BGM]
[ONLY] One lead in a matte-black suit and one creature. No other people, no vehicles, no on-screen text.

[Reference]
@Image2 = START FRAME. The first frame matches this image exactly.
@Image1 = DESIGN ONLY: suit, helmet, build. Ignore its pose and angle.
@Image3 = DESIGN ONLY: creature body, color, spear-arm. Ignore its pose and angle.
REFERENCES ARE NOT FRAMES: none of these images may appear as a frame anywhere in the video.

[Character]
IDENTITY LOCK: lead = slim build, never bulky. Matte-black suit, no glow. Closed round helmet, large visor, no hair visible. Seen only from behind or rear 3/4 for the whole cut.

[Others]
LEAD 1 + CREATURE 1 = 2 bodies total. Creature starts screen-left, 6 m away, spear-arm raised. Larger than the lead.

[Global]
Photoreal live action, ARRI Alexa + anamorphic, film grain, practical rain and sparks. Stunt-realistic bodies, impossible camera choreography. Speed lives in the background and the camera, not in the body.

[Cut]
0.0–0.8s  Hold on the start frame. Rain streaks. Lead's shoulders drop, chest rises twice. Creature's spear-arm twitches.
0.8–1.0s  HARD CUT to a ground-level side angle. Creature lunges, spear-arm thrust at the lead's chest.
1.0–1.3s  Lead slides one body-width to screen-right, boots skidding, fan of water. The thrust misses and punches through the container behind him — 1-frame white flash, 3-frame hit-stop, steel panel folds inward, sparks and rain blast outward.
1.3–2.5s  Whip-pan follows the slide; camera orbits 90° behind the lead. He plants: knees bent, hips above knee height, never sits.
2.5–3.0s  HARD CUT to a high angle. The lead's forearm blade ignites: red fire, a trail follows every movement.
3.0–3.4s  Lead drives forward two body-lengths; the blade sweeps upward through the creature's spear-arm — 1-frame white flash, 2-frame hit-stop. The arm shears; the creature's whole body leaves the ground, flies back 3 m and embeds in the container stack. The fire trail hangs in the air for 10 frames.
3.4–5.5s  Camera whip-tilts down to the lead, 15° dutch, pushing in on his back. Trail still burning. Rain hisses on the blade.
5.5–8.0s  Settle into the end frame: low rear 3/4 on the lead, blade still lit, creature embedded top-right, pieces still falling. Not the first composition.

[Camera]
Locked on the start frame for 0.8 s. Every beat after the first hard cut has a move: ground-level side, 90° orbit, high angle, whip-tilt, dutch push-in. The camera never moves in front of the lead's visor.

[Look]
Night, sodium-orange yard lights, cold blue rain. Heavy grain. Red fire is the only warm highlight.

[Sound]
Rain on steel, boots skidding, one deep impact, metal folding, fire hiss, falling debris. No music, no dialogue.

FAIL if: the lead's face or visor is shown frontally · the lead stays in place · only the torso twists · the creature flies off without being cut · a vehicle or extra appears · the blade fire goes out after the sweep · the last frame returns to the first composition · any reference image appears as a frame.
```

## Fight-rules check

No "hits / strikes" sentence · lead moves from → to in every beat · one thing breaks per beat · camera move per beat · flash + hit-stop + result on both contacts · end ≠ start · 2 big moves in 8 s · one creature, one face · under 10,000 characters.

전투법칙 체크 통과: 때린다 문장 없음, 비트마다 이동·파괴·카메라·히트스톱, 끝 구도 다름, 8초에 큰 동작 2개.
